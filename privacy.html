Privacy policy

Last updated 24 November 2026

omarchy-gmail is a personal tool used only by its owner, with the owner's own Google account.

  • What it accesses: the Gmail messages and labels of the signed-in account, through Google's Gmail API, to show and manage the inbox on the owner's desktop.
  • Where data goes: nowhere else. Mail is fetched directly from Google to the owner's computer. No server run by this app receives it, and it is not sold, shared or used for advertising.
  • Storage: sign-in tokens are kept encrypted on the owner's computer. Mail is not stored beyond the app's short-lived display cache.
  • Revoking access: remove the app at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
